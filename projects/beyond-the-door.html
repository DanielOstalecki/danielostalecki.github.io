Beyond The Door

Prototype
University Team Project (Year 1)

Beyond The Door is a horror prototype developed during my first university year in Game Design. The story follows a father who, consumed by work, neglects his family—and the evil that hides behind it. Trapped in a time loop, he must uncover what is happening and why.
