Dimitria

Prototype
University Team Project (Capstone)

Dimitria is a local co-op prototype developed as the final university project of our Game Design program. The story follows two videogame-loving boys transported into a fantasy world—only through cooperation can they uncover why they ended up there.

Quick facts

Project type
University / Capstone Team project Local co-op
Engine
Unreal Engine
Scope
~30 min playable prototype

My role

Game Designer · Narrative Designer · Programmer

I contributed to gameplay and narrative design, and implemented parts of the prototype in Unreal Engine to validate mechanics and interaction flow.

Design focus

Co-op mechanics Narrative-driven gameplay Player clarity & onboarding Iteration

Key contributions

  • Designed cooperative mechanics that require coordination between the two players.
  • Supported narrative structure and ensured gameplay moments aligned with story beats.
  • Implemented parts of the prototype in Unreal Engine to test mechanics and interaction flow.
  • Iterated on pacing and readability based on internal feedback and playtests.
